Loan Programs

Find the program that fits your situation.

Conventional

Conforming

Loans that meet Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac guidelines, including the annual loan limits. They typically offer competitive rates for borrowers with solid credit.

Non-Conforming

Conventional loans that fall outside agency guidelines, such as unique property or borrower profiles.

Conventional Refi

Replace your current mortgage with a new conventional loan to lower your rate, change your term, or take cash out of your equity.

Government

FHA

Insured by the Federal Housing Administration. Down payments can be as low as 3.5% and credit requirements are more flexible, making it popular with first-time buyers.

VA

Backed by the Department of Veterans Affairs for eligible veterans, service members, and surviving spouses. Often requires no down payment and no monthly mortgage insurance.

Non-QM

DSCR Loans

Designed for investors. Qualification is based on the rental property's income compared to its debt payments (debt service coverage ratio), not your personal income.

Bank Statement Loans

For self-employed borrowers. Income is verified using 12 to 24 months of bank statements instead of tax returns.

Foreign National Loans

For non-U.S. citizens purchasing U.S. property who may not have U.S. credit history or a Social Security number.

P&L Loans

Income is verified with a profit and loss statement prepared by a CPA or tax professional, ideal for business owners with write-offs.

Jumbo

Jumbo Purchase

Financing for home purchases above conforming loan limits, common in higher-priced markets.

Jumbo Refinance

Refinance an existing high-balance mortgage to improve your rate or terms, or access equity.

Reverse

HECM

The FHA-insured Home Equity Conversion Mortgage for homeowners 62 and older. Turn home equity into funds with no required monthly mortgage payment. The loan is repaid when the home is sold, the borrower moves out, or passes away. Borrowers must still pay property taxes, insurance, and upkeep.
